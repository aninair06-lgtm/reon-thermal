      Worst day: what keeps every cell under 60 °C

      40 °C site · 55 °C start · one aged cell
      Peak temperature of the hottest cell after 2 hours at full power (30 kW), when the cabinet already starts the discharge at 55 °C. Green bars stay under the 60 °C limit.

      What works: start the cabinet cooler, make less heat, or take the aged cell out. Two together is safest.

      What falls short: moving heat out during the discharge. From a 55 °C start, heat can't leave the cells fast enough.
